# Quizzes rc.26

Desktop projector multiple-choice and true/false questions without image/audio have a new stage inspired by the chosen concept: a large white question card, dark purple show lighting, centre timer and four vibrant answer tiles. Six options use a three-column layout. Questions with image/audio, buzzer rounds and answer reveals retain their established layouts. The player phone screen is unchanged. The host console remains functional and separate; it is not the projector design shown in the sample.

The projector does not show the number of answers during an open question. The rc.25 buzzer fixes are included. No schema change, migration, or alteration of saved quizzes, assignments, publications, attempts or scores.
