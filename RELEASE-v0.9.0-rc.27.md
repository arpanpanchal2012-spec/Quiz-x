# Quizzes rc.27

- Answer reveals use vote count and percentage badges on answer tiles, with a highlighted correct answer. No answer counts are shown during an open question. The reveal retains the question and options and removes the attached image. For multi-answer questions, percentages are shares of all selections, not percentages of participants.
- More noticeable projector round and last-question announcements, preserving custom round names and the host's start control.
- A polished final projector podium with animated character figures and legible names and points; other standings do not show faces or characters.
- Projector buzzer queue has larger names and elapsed times; host queue is more readable too. Buzzer order and scoring are unchanged from rc.25.
- This release includes rc.25's buzzer repairs and rc.26's question stage. No migrations or changes to stored quizzes, publications, attempts or scores.
